# Unit Economics

## What a Converted Lead is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits. Average startup buying credits:
- Average deal size: $500/month in credits
- Gross margin on credits: ~20%
- Monthly gross profit per customer: $100
- Average customer lifetime: 12 months
- LTV per customer: $1,200

## CAC at Each Channel

| Channel | Est. CAC |
|---------|----------|
| Reddit/HN organic post | $0 (time only) |
| Twitter thread | $0 (time only) |

| Product Hunt launch | $0 (time only) |
| LinkedIn DMs | $0 (time only) |
| Paid Twitter ads (later) | ~$50-100/lead |

For organic channels, CAC is effectively $0 cash — just founder time.

## Conversion Funnel Math

- 1000 audits completed
- 400 emails captured (40% conversion)
- 80 book a Credex consultation (20% of emails)
- 16 become paying customers (20% of consultations)
- Revenue: 16 x $500/mo = $8,000 MRR from 1000 audits

**This means: every 62 audits = 1 paying customer**

## What Makes This Profitable

The tool costs ~$0/month to run on free tiers:
- Vercel: free
- Supabase: free up to 50,000 rows
- Anthropic API: ~$0.01 per summary

At 1000 audits/month, total cost is under $50/month.
First paying customer covers 6 months of costs.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months

**What has to be true:**

Month 1-3: 500 audits/month, 8 customers, $4,000 MRR
Month 4-6: 2,000 audits/month via Product Hunt + word of mouth, 32 customers, $16,000 MRR
Month 7-12: 5,000 audits/month via SEO + partnerships, 80 customers, $40,000 MRR
Month 13-18: 20,000 audits/month, 320 customers, $160,000 MRR

To hit $1M ARR = $83,333 MRR = ~167 active customers paying $500/month

**Required:** 167 customers in 18 months = ~10 new customers/month by month 12

This is achievable if:
- Audit completion rate stays above 40%
- Email capture rate stays above 35%
- Consultation booking rate stays above 15%
- Sales close rate stays above 20%

These are conservative rates for a tool that shows real dollar savings upfront.
