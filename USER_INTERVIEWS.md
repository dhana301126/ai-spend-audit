# User Interviews

## Interview 1

**Name:** Anonymous (shared initials only)
**Role:** Student
**Company stage:** Individual user

**Summary:**
Spoke about using Gemini, Cursor, NotebookLM and Claude for studying and work.
Company pays for most tools. Pays ~$200/month personally for Gemini as a student.
Finds it useful for studying.

**Direct Quotes:**
- "Company pays for these AI tools"
- "I am because I'm a student and it's useful for studying"
- "Yes, raising price" (when asked if they'd switch due to price increases)

**Most surprising thing:**
They were okay with the company paying but personally spending $200/month as a 
student on Gemini was surprising — shows how sticky these tools become even for 
non-professional users.

**What it changed about my design:**
Added a student/individual use case consideration. The tool currently focuses on 
startups but individual power users are also overpaying. Added "research" and 
"mixed" use cases to the form to better serve this segment.

## Interview 2

**Name:** Anonymous
**Role:** Individual user / professional
**Company stage:** Individual

**Summary:**
Pays 799/month for ChatGPT. Uses it for getting accurate answers without 
unnecessary extra content.

**Direct Quotes:**
- "799 — I pay for ChatGPT"
- "I don't think so" (when asked if they feel they're overpaying)
- "Yes I do" (when asked if they get value)
- "If it gives accurate answer exactly what I need and how much I need without extra unnecessary matter"

**Most surprising thing:**
They defined value purely as answer accuracy and conciseness — not features or 
integrations. This was unexpected. Most product thinking focuses on features 
but this user just wants precise answers.

**What it changed about my design:**
The audit results now emphasize answer quality comparisons between tools, 
not just price. Added reasoning about use-case fit, not just cost savings.

## Interview 3

**Name:** Anonymous
**Role:** Student
**Company stage:** Individual user

**Summary:**
Uses Gemini for college work. Gets it free through student Google account.
Happy with it because there is no cost involved.

**Direct Quotes:**
- "Gemini"
- "Its free for my student id"
- "I feel good because its free"

**Most surprising thing:**
Students are getting AI tools completely free through institutional accounts 
and don't think about cost at all. This is a blind spot in the product — 
the tool assumes people are paying, but a whole segment gets it free and 
will only start paying when they graduate and lose access.

**What it changed about my design:**
Added a "Free plan" option for every tool in the form so students and 
free tier users can still audit whether they should upgrade or not.
