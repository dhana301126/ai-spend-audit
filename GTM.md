# Go-To-Market Plan

## Exact Target User
Engineering Manager or CTO at a 5-20 person seed-stage startup.
They manage a team using multiple AI tools, they approved the subscriptions,
and they feel vague guilt about the bill but haven't audited it.
They are not the CFO — they don't have a spreadsheet. They just pay the invoice.

## What They Google Before Wanting This Tool
- "is cursor worth it for teams"
- "chatgpt team vs enterprise pricing"
- "github copilot alternatives 2026"
- "how much should a startup spend on AI tools"
- "cursor vs github copilot for small team"

## Where They Hang Out Online
- r/ExperiencedDevs
- r/startups
- r/SideProject
- Hacker News (Show HN, Ask HN)
- Indie Hackers community
- Local founder Slack groups
- Twitter/X — following @levelsio, @paulg, @shreyas

## First 100 Users in 30 Days — $0 Budget

**Week 1:**
- Post on r/startups: "I built a free tool that audits your AI tool spend — found $400/mo in savings for my own team"
- Post on Hacker News Show HN
- Share in 3 founder Slack groups (Indie Hackers, Online Geniuses, Product Hunt Makers)

**Week 2:**
- DM 50 CTOs on LinkedIn who follow AI tool accounts
- Post a Twitter thread: "We audited our AI tool spend and found we were paying for 3 overlapping tools. Here's what we found..."
- Submit to daily.dev and Hashnode

**Week 3:**
- Reach out to 10 startup newsletters for a feature (TLDR, Bytes, Startup Curated)
- Post in IndieHackers "What I built this week"

**Week 4:**
- Product Hunt launch
- Ask first 50 users to share their audit result URL on Twitter

## Unfair Distribution Channel
Credex already has relationships with startup founders who buy discounted AI credits.
Every existing Credex customer is a perfect user for this tool — and every tool user
is a warm lead for Credex credits. The existing customer base is the unfair advantage
no competitor can replicate without building Credex first.
